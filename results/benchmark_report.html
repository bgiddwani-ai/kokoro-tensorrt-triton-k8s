Serving load and sustained latency

The selected four-worker configuration completed 1,954 requests in 30.072 seconds: p50 53.79 ms, p95 105.61 ms, p99 113.95 ms, maximum 120.36 ms, and zero errors.

The historical matrix peak was 69.05 requests/s with Hybrid + MPS · 8 workers, concurrency 32. This short-cell peak is separate from the sustained result. Matrix cells contain 96–384 requests with variable elapsed time. Four workers at concurrency 4 were selected to balance throughput and latency.

All 12/12 final served passage checks were finite. Maximum absolute duration change: 0.287%. Matched Whisper small.en CPU-int8 transcription was used for both backends. These scores check intelligibility on this small corpus; they do not establish waveform, speaker, or prosody identity.

Tested FP16/mixed candidates produced non-finite audio and were rejected. BF16 was finite but slower than the FP32 candidate. Native TensorRT was slower on longer passages; retaining LSTMs on CUDA/cuDNN improved the selected path. CUDA graph capture was not used because of predictor control flow.

Follow the repository's three-step README: build the single image, apply k8s.yaml, and run:

kubectl -n kokoro exec "$POD" -- python3 -m kokoro_triton.run_benchmarks
kubectl -n kokoro cp "$POD:/data/benchmark" ./benchmark-output

The benchmark pauses serving for isolated eager/hybrid measurements, reloads the model, then runs the selected serving matrix, sustained check, and audio checks. Historical native candidates and worker sweeps are retained as results, not additional deployment variants.
